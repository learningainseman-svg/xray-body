# X-Ray Body Mirror

Point a phone or laptop camera at a person and see a 3D skeleton, organs and blood circulation follow their movements, with every part named.

It shows typical anatomy scaled and posed by body tracking. It is not a scan of the person and not a medical or diagnostic tool. Video is processed on the device and never uploaded.
